Input contract & time rules

Each event has a unique id, start, end, and optional label. Use ISO dates with Z or an explicit ±HH:MM offset. Dates must exist; end must be later than start.

Intervals include their start and exclude their end: [start, end). Events are clipped to the window. Adjacent events do not conflict. Export sorts events and converts timestamps to UTC; it preserves instants, not the original offset notation.

Offsets are supplied facts. This tool does not infer a location, daylight-saving rules, recurrence or all-day events.
